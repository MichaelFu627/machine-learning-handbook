## The idea

Training data fits parameters. Validation data helps choose models and hyperparameters. A final test set estimates performance after those choices. Fit preprocessing only on training data and apply the fitted transformation to the other splits.

## The mathematics

$$
\mathrm{Precision}=\frac{TP}{TP+FP},\quad\mathrm{Recall}=\frac{TP}{TP+FN}
$$

## Try this experiment

In the classifier laboratories, the held-out score updates as you tune. Treat it as a validation score, even if a library convention calls the split test. A real final test set must stay untouched during this exploration.

## Know the limits

Accuracy can hide rare-class failure. Compare precision and recall when mistakes have different costs. Split groups together and use chronological splits for time-dependent data. Repeated validation tuning can also overfit.
