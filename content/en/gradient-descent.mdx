## The idea

The gradient points toward the steepest local increase. Subtract it to move downhill. The learning rate controls the distance of each move. This quadratic is a small optimization laboratory rather than a fitted regression model.

## The mathematics

$$
\theta_{t+1}=\theta_t-\eta\nabla J(\theta_t),\quad J(x,y)=x^2+2y^2
$$

## Try this experiment

Play with rates 0.1, 0.3, and 0.55. Use the single-step control to compare stable descent, alternating coordinates, and divergence. The warning is computed from the actual update rule.

## Know the limits

For this quadratic the update factors are 1−2η and 1−4η. Both must have magnitude below one for convergence from arbitrary starting points: $0<\eta<0.5$. Other functions have other limits.
