## The idea

Regularization trades some training fit for constrained coefficients. Ridge shrinks smoothly. Lasso can make a coefficient exactly zero. Feature scaling matters because the penalty acts on coefficient size.

## The mathematics

$$
\min_w\frac{1}{2n}\|y-Xw\|^2+\lambda R(w),\quad R_1=\|w\|_1,\ R_2=\|w\|_2^2
$$

## Try this experiment

Increase λ and follow both coefficient paths. Both methods use the displayed normalized objective. The same numerical λ does not imply that L1 and L2 impose equivalent constraints.

## Know the limits

Choose λ using validation data or cross-validation; do not select it on the final test set. A zero Lasso coefficient is not evidence of no causal effect. For sklearn Ridge this convention uses alpha=2nλ; Lasso uses alpha=λ.
