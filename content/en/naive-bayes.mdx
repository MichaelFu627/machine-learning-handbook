## The idea

Gaussian Naive Bayes estimates a mean and variance for every feature in every class. The naive assumption is conditional independence of features given the class. The resulting class scores combine log priors and log likelihoods.

## The mathematics

$$
P(y=k\mid x)\propto P(y=k)\prod_j P(x_j\mid y=k)
$$

## Try this experiment

Compare linear, moon, and circle datasets. Ask whether independent per-feature Gaussian distributions describe each class. Change variance smoothing and inspect validation performance.

## Know the limits

Conditional independence is not unconditional independence. Gaussian NB is one variant; count and binary data often motivate Multinomial or Bernoulli NB. Accurate class labels do not guarantee calibrated probabilities.
