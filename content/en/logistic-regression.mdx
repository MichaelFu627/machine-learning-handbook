## The idea

Logistic regression models log-odds as a linear function. Sigmoid maps the score to a number between zero and one. Fitting minimizes binary cross-entropy plus an optional regularizer. This laboratory uses gradient descent with an L2 penalty on weights, not the intercept.

## The mathematics

$$
p(y=1\mid x)=\sigma(w^Tx+b)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-(w^Tx+b)}}
$$

## Know the limits

A probability is not a guarantee. A threshold of 0.5 is a convention, not a universal optimum. A straight boundary can fail on curved patterns, and regularization does not repair the wrong model family.
