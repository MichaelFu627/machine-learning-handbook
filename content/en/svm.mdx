## The idea

SVM balances a large margin against margin violations. Support vectors influence the fitted boundary. Kernels evaluate inner products in a feature space without explicitly constructing all its coordinates. The laboratory standardizes inputs using training data only.

## The mathematics

$$
\min_{w,b,\xi}\frac12\|w\|^2+C\sum_i\xi_i,\quad y_i(w^T\phi(x_i)+b)\ge1-\xi_i
$$

## Try this experiment

Compare a linear kernel with RBF on circles. Increase gamma to make an RBF response more local. Increase C to penalize margin violations more strongly, then compare validation accuracy.

## Know the limits

Large C does not make inseparable data separable. Decision values are scores, not calibrated probabilities or automatically normalized geometric distances. The theory uses labels −1/+1; displayed labels 0/1 are recoded internally.
